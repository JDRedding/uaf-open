## 🐐 Minimal Goat Example (v0.1)

This tiny model demonstrates the **full causal chain**:

- **Capability**: protect garden  
- **Operational**: prevent goat entry  
- **Realization**: fence + boundary protection service  
- **Actual**: west‑plot fence installation  

And it includes:

- a performer  
- an exchange  
- a constraint  
- typed relations  
- evidence linkage  
- ASCII‑stable IDs  
- no vendor‑specific constructs  

It is the smallest example that still behaves like a real architecture.
